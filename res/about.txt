BlueXLogger 1.0.0.0
Created by Xencode-CLI by xanthorox

A Windows keystroke and screen-capture agent with batched Gmail and
Telegram delivery.

Components
  BlueXLogger.exe    capture payload (configured by the builder)
  BlueXBuilder.exe   graphical configuration builder
